# Monopoly Deal

## Register

product

## Users
Friends playing together on computers and phones, locally or over a shared link. Solo players practice against one to four robots. The repository supports spectators, multilingual tables, and chat.

## Product Purpose
Make the server-authoritative Monopoly Deal rules feel like a lively shared game night. Build three complete property sets while reading opponents, playing actions, and defending against debts and steals. Preserve every existing rules interaction and private-hand boundary.

## Brand Personality
Playful, dimensional, competitive. The user explicitly references Ubisoft UNO's shared space, animation, card interactions and personality. Desktop is the primary surface; touch and landscape phones remain supported.

## Anti-references
The existing boxed green panels and timid card transitions read as a 2007 browser card game. Avoid a dashboard of identical player panels, tiny primary cards, and decoration that hides game state.

## Design Principles
- Put everyone around one shared table.
- Make card movement explain what happened.
- Give the hand, active player, and next action the clearest hierarchy.
- Keep chat, full opponent boards, and settings within reach without consuming the table.
- Preserve the existing English, Italian, and German catalogs and offline operation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Implementation defaults: keyboard-accessible card actions, visible focus, written color and state labels, reduced-motion support, and touch alternatives to drag and drop. No additional user-specific requirements have been supplied.
